Health scores based on expected vs actual consumption deviation

❤️ Health Scores

Health is calculated by comparing actual consumption with expected values. Persistent deviation over multiple days flags a possible fault.

📋 Health Thresholds

Deviation RangeStatusAction
0–10%NormalNo action needed
10–20%MonitorKeep an eye on usage
20–30%WarningInspect appliance
Above 30%Possible FaultService recommended
