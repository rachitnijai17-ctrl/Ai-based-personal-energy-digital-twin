📐 Energy Formula

Energy (kWh) = Power (Watts) ÷ 1000 × Hours
Example: AC (1500W) running for 8.5 hours = 1500 ÷ 1000 × 8.5 = 12.75 kWh
Cost (₹) = Energy (kWh) × Tariff (₹/kWh) = 12.75 × 8 = ₹102
